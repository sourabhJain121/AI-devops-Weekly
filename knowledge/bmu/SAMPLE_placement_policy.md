## 1. Eligibility for Placement

A student of the SAMPLE university is eligible to participate in campus placement if
all of the following are satisfied:

- A minimum cumulative CGPA of 6.5 on a 10-point scale at the time of application.
- No more than two active backlogs at the time of the recruitment drive.
- Completion of at least 75% attendance in the current semester.
- Registration with the Placement Office before the announced cut-off date.

## 2. Backlog Rules

A student carrying more than two active backlogs is not permitted to sit for any
placement drive until the backlogs are cleared. Backlogs cleared through a supplementary
examination count as cleared from the date the result is published.

## 3. Application Rules

- A student may hold only one active offer at a time.
- After accepting an offer, the student is withdrawn from all further drives.
- A student who does not attend a drive after registering for it may be barred from
  the next two drives.

## 4. Documents Required

Students must submit the following to the Placement Office before registration:

- Updated resume in PDF format
- Consolidated marksheet of all completed semesters
- Government-issued photo identification
- Signed placement policy acknowledgement form

## 5. Placement Restrictions

Students who have already accepted a pre-placement offer arising from an internship are
not eligible for further placement drives in the same academic year, except where the
Placement Office grants written permission.
